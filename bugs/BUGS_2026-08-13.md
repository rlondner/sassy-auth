  seed writes are **absent on `master`** and present on #316/#315.

  with no mention in title/body; test diffs add `Tooltip: Passthrough` mocks.
- **bug-0252** static-observed from the diff (identical in all 5 tables); behavioral repro not
  yet run — kept low-confidence.
- **bug-0249 retracted.** Initial "missing provider" reasoning traced the Radix source correctly

  duplicates the 2026-08-04 false positive; recorded in `bug-0249.md` so it isn't re-filed.
